Main Study Protocol — Brief Overview

Research Questions

Main RQ: How does GenAI-based metacognitive scaffolding manifest in the everyday task management practices of people with executive function difficulties, and what design implications does it reveal?

  • Sub-RQ1: How do people with executive function difficulties invoke, interpret, and negotiate GenAI-based metacognitive scaffolding in everyday task management?
  • Sub-RQ2: What key design principles and boundaries for GenAI-based metacognitive scaffolding are revealed through these use experiences?

Study Design

ParadigmQualitative-dominant mixed-methods field study
Duration~2.5 weeks (Day 0 + 14-day deployment + Day 15)
Participants12–16 university students with ADHD diagnosis or significant executive function difficulties
PrototypeMetaPlan — a desktop task management tool with GenAI-based metacognitive scaffolding

Day 0 — Pre-study Session

Pre-interview: Baseline Collection

Goal: Understand participants' task management practices before using MetaPlan. This serves as the "before" reference for later analysis.

Core topics (~20–30 min):

  1. Current daily practice — What tasks do you typically handle? How do you decide what to do first? What tools or strategies do you use?
  2. Key difficulties — What is the biggest challenge in managing tasks? What do you do when you get stuck or distracted mid-task?
  3. Prior tool experience — Have you tried any task management tools before? What worked / didn't work? What would you most want a tool to help with?

No separate baseline period — participants' existing habits collected through the pre-interview serve as the reference point.

Day 1–14 — 2-week Deployment

Minimum Usage Requirements

RequirementThreshold
Use MetaPlan≥ 4 days per week
Reflection conversations≥ 3 per week
Micro-survey response rate≥ 70% (≥ 10 out of 14 days)

Daily Micro-survey (5 items, 7-point Likert, ~1 min)

#ItemDimension
1I knew clearly what to do first todayForethought
2When stuck, I could figure out how to continuePerformance control
3I had a clear sense of how I spent my time todayMonitoring
4I am satisfied with my task completion todaySelf-reflection
5Using MetaPlan helped me manage my tasks todayTool perception

Researcher Role

  • Passive observation + usage monitoring (no feature-specific nudging)
  • Technical support when needed
  • Friendly reminder if usage falls below minimum threshold
  • Mid-check at Day 7–8: brief, non-directive message

Analysis Approach

(quantitative)		 • Pre/post questionnaire: Wilcoxon signed-rank test (small N)

Key Design Decisions & Rationale

DecisionRationale
No separate baseline periodPre-interview captures existing habits; avoids extra burden on ADHD participants
2-week deploymentAllows habit formation and pattern emergence beyond novelty effect
Minimum usage requirementsEnsures sufficient data for analysis while keeping expectations low
Trace elicitation in post interviewGrounds discussion in actual behavior, reduces recall bias
Qualitative-dominantSmall N; goal is understanding "how" and "why", not measuring effect size
